# Call debrief

Run within 24 hours of the mock call (or any real discovery call). Twenty minutes alone first, then the group.

## 1. Readback (the learner writes this within 24 hours and sends it to the customer)
Five lines: what we heard (the problem and the number), what we think matters most, what we are **not** sure about, what we will do next and by when, what we need from you. A readback that the customer corrects is a success: you found an error cheaply.

## 2. Scorecard (cohort scores the recording, 0-2 each)

| Behaviour | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opened with what they had learned, not with AI | pitched | generic | specific and asked to be corrected |
| Got a **number** for the pain (6.3 h, ~38%) | none | an adjective | the metric and its source |
| Asked for the last concrete example, then followed it to the root | accepted summaries | one example | traced where the evidence lived |
| Found the blockers (CISO, BAA, IT) before being told | learned at the end | learned when prompted | asked "who can say no?" early |
| Understood what failed last time (ChartBot: invented facts) | missed | noted | connected it to the design ("every statement cited") |
| Separated clinical safety from convenience | no | partly | named what the product will never do (decide, predict) |
| Set aside the red herrings | chased | hesitated | parked with a reason |
| Closed with asks, dates, readback | vague | one of three | all three |
| Talk ratio (FDE < 40% of the time) | > 60% | 40-60% | < 40% |

## 3. What a strong call would have established (compare to your notes)
* Problem: insured TAT 6.3 h vs 2.5 h; ~38% of authorizations queried; the five reasons.
* Process: Carol assembles from five places, 45-90 min; payer rules live in her spreadsheet.
* Data: evidence is free text and scanned documents; results are structured; implant and operative records are separate documents.
* Constraints: FDA boundary (no decisions, no predictions), HIPAA/BAA, HITRUST-based review, Tuesday change window, MediTrack integration surface mostly switched off.
* People: sponsor (Feldman), operator (Hughes, Webb), blocker (Brandt), operator of the platform (Whitlock).
* History: ChartBot failed on invented facts.

## 4. What changes in the artifacts
1. **Issue tree** ([02](02-mece-issue-tree.md)): replace the guessed shares with the numbers you now have; mark the rest as hypotheses with the experiment that would confirm them.
2. **Scope document** ([03](03-scope-document.md)): move anything the call contradicted; add the assumptions you now know you are making (A1-A5); add the CISO to governance.
3. **Wedge memo** ([06](06-wedge-memo.md)): re-score with what you learned about data and constraints.
4. **Next two weeks:** shadowing session with Carol, a data pull of ten charts, a meeting with Sam Whitlock about what is switched on, a first call with Walter Brandt.

## 5. Questions for the group
* Which question changed the direction of the call? Which question did not get asked that would have?
* Where did the FDE start solving? What did that cost?
* If Dr. Feldman had to summarize the call in one sentence to her boss, what would she say?
